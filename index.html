01 / MOTIVATION & GOALS

A new future for
masternode &
staking coins.

Reborn Chains aims to revive abandoned coin projects and bring masternode and staking technologies to a wider audience.

Many projects still have committed coin holders, a community and technical foundations, even after the original developers have stepped away. We want to build on what remains.

Our belief: as long as the source code exists, the possibility of a new beginning remains. Whether and how a project can be revived must be assessed individually, both technically and economically.

Our vision goes beyond restarting a blockchain. We aim to develop globally accessible payment infrastructure that gives these coins practical utility through mobile payments, everyday applications and gradual integration into real-world payment processes.

Over the long term, we want to help develop a global market for masternode and staking coins. Our focus is on reliable technical foundations, active communities and applications that meet lasting needs. These are the building blocks of a more resilient ecosystem.

Coin holders, investors, community members and developers are invited to help shape this future. Together, we aim to preserve existing potential and turn it into new opportunities.

Our goal is long-term development with real utility. Technical progress and broader adoption cannot guarantee stable prices, commercial success or returns.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

A clear path forward.

Six proposed stages.
One project-specific agreement.

  1. 01

    Propose a coin

    Coin holders, investors and community members can propose a coin by social media direct message or in the dedicated coin submission channel on Discord. Include the coin’s name, ticker, source-code link and available project details.

  2. 02

    Project review

    We assess technical feasibility, available infrastructure and community readiness. Submission does not guarantee acceptance.

  3. 03

    Agree the contribution

    The proposed contribution is one masternode, plus an additional negotiable amount of coins. Collateral, transfer arrangements and all terms must be agreed in writing before any contribution.

  4. 04

    Launch & explorer integration

    Work towards launching the agreed program and integrating a block explorer, subject to the chain’s technical condition and available resources.

  5. 05

    Exchange listing

    Pursue a suitable exchange listing where feasible. Approval, timing, fees and continued availability depend on the exchange; no listing is promised.

  6. 06

    Genuine trading activity

    Support awareness, utility and voluntary market participation. No wash trading, artificial volume or price manipulation. Trading interest and liquidity cannot be guaranteed.

The sequence is indicative. Scope, responsibilities, costs and milestones are defined for each accepted project.

03 / BUYBACK PROGRAM

Support with
a longer horizon.

PROGRAM ACTIVE

A project-specific buyback may form part of an agreed revival plan.

The Buyback Program has launched. Participation and any purchases are subject to project-specific terms covering funding, eligible coins, purchase method, limits and reporting. An active program does not mean that purchases are underway for every proposed coin.

Buybacks depend on available resources and market conditions. They do not establish a price floor, guarantee liquidity or promise a return, and may be changed, paused or discontinued under the published terms.
